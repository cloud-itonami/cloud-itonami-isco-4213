# Business Model: Independent Pawnbroking & Small-Loan Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-4213`
- ISCO-08: `4213`
- Occupation: Pawnbrokers and Money-lenders
- Social impact: financial-inclusion, consumer-protection, local-jobs

## Customer

- individuals needing short-term secured loans
- collectors and resellers

## Offer

- collateral appraisal
- short-term secured loan
- collateral storage
- loan redemption/forfeiture processing

## Revenue

- loan interest
- appraisal fee
- storage fee

## Trust Controls

- no loan disbursement above the item's registered appraised-value ceiling without governor gate
- collateral must be registered and appraised before any loan offer
- loan and redemption records are auditable, not editable
